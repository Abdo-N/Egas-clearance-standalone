1. Purpose, roles, and workflow

The system replaces the manual movement of a paper clearance form. Document and Records Management creates a request; 13 departments review it; Document and Records Management checks the collected evidence; and IT performs the final access-removal action.

Department sequence

Departments 1–11 work in parallel. IT is department 10 and requires five separate checklist items. Wages and Entitlements (12) and Financial Affairs (13) unlock together only after every Tier-1 department is complete.

A signed form is not yet completed. A request changes to “completed” only after all departments have signed, Document and Records Management has approved the evidence, and IT confirms that system access was removed.

2. Getting started

Actual login screen. The demo account picker is a development aid and may not appear in production.

  1. Open the website URL supplied by IT.
  2. Enter your work email and password. There is no self-registration. On your very first login, this is the exact password the Admin entered when creating your account — it is already your real, permanent password, not a placeholder, and the site will not force you to change it.
  3. Select Log in. The system opens the dashboard for your role automatically.
  4. Use the language, light/dark theme, and logout controls in the top-right area.

If you want to change your password

There is no self-service "change password" option. To get a different password, ask your Admin to reset your account — an Admin, not IT, since IT no longer resets or deletes accounts.

Temporary password after an admin reset

An admin-issued reset password (as opposed to the one set at account creation) is temporary. Log in by entering the one-time password provided by the Admin. The website then redirects you to Set new password. Choose a new permanent password of at least 12 characters containing a symbol, confirm it, then sign in normally.

Password confirmation

Signing, reopening, approving, deleting, resetting an account, and removing access require your current password again. This proves that the person at the keyboard is still the logged-in user.

Forgotten password

Select Forgot your password? or Need help? on the login screen to find an Admin or Super Admin contact. There is no email reset link.

Never share credentials or signature evidence. Always log out on a shared workstation. Contact an administrator if your account, department, or IT checklist assignment is wrong.

3. Document and Records Management

FILE MANAGEMENT

You own the shared operational queue, from request creation through evidence approval and PDF download.

Actual Document and Records Management dashboard.

Create a clearance request

  1. Select File a new clearance request.
  2. Enter the employee’s full name, employee number, job title, and directorate/assistant directorate.
  3. Select the leaving reason and suggested last working day.
  4. Check spelling and the employee number carefully. These details appear throughout the process and on the clearance PDF.
  5. Select File request. The system snapshots the current 13-department configuration onto the request.

The actual request form is visible in the dashboard. Required choices are populated by the application configuration.

Before submitting: confirm the employee number is correct, choose the most precise leaving reason, and verify the last working day. If the request itself is wrong, contact Document and Records Management before any department signs. A request can only be permanently deleted after it reaches completed status; incorrect active requests require the approved operational correction process.

Evidence review checklist

  • Employee details on the request are correct.
  • Every department shows completed.
  • Each image or PDF is legible and belongs to the correct department/item.
  • Wages and Finance notes, if present, are understood.
  • The composited clearance PDF is readable.

If evidence is unclear, select the affected department or IT item and use Reopen, then re-enter your password. Reopening clears that signature/evidence and also revokes any earlier File Management approval. The responsible reviewer must sign again with corrected evidence.

When all evidence is satisfactory, choose Approve and confirm with your current password. Approval does not complete the request; IT’s final access-removal action still follows.

4. Department reviewers

REVIEWER

A reviewer acts only for their assigned department. Standard departments use one signature per request; multiple reviewers in the same department share responsibility, but one valid sign-off completes that department.

Actual Security reviewer dashboard with summary metrics and signed work.

Sign a request

  1. Open a request under Waiting on your department.
  2. Verify the employee name, number, role, leaving reason, and last working day.
  3. Complete your department’s offline checks according to EGAS policy.
  4. Upload evidence in JPG, PNG, or PDF format. Use a clear scan/photo of the authorized signature or stamp.
  5. Enter your current password and confirm the signature.
  6. Verify that the request moves to Already signed by your department.

If you made a mistake

Before the request is finally completed, the responsible reviewer may reopen their own department and sign again. Reopening removes the stored sign-off and evidence. It also invalidates any File Management approval so the replacement is reviewed.

Privacy boundary: ordinary reviewers see only their own department slice. Wages and Finance are deliberate exceptions because they have organization-wide oversight responsibility.
Locked request? Wages and Finance remain locked until all Tier-1 departments finish. Ordinary departments should be available immediately unless the request is already final.

Actual IT dashboard. The waiting request is ready for the final access-removal action in this demo dataset.

Complete your assigned item

  1. Open the request waiting on your item.
  2. Perform the applicable IT check.
  3. Upload clear evidence, enter your password, and sign.
  4. If you uploaded the wrong evidence, reopen your own IT item and repeat.

Final access-removal action

After every department signs and Document and Records Management approves, any IT reviewer can perform the final action. Confirm that the employee’s access has actually been removed, select the final action, and re-enter your password. Only then does the request become completed.

Do not use the final action as a placeholder. It is an auditable confirmation that access removal really occurred. If the dashboard says prerequisites are missing, resolve those prerequisites rather than attempting to bypass them.

6. Wages and Finance oversight

TIER 2 REVIEWER

Wages and Entitlements and Financial Affairs unlock together after all Tier-1 work is complete. Both roles can see the full 13-department status, signer details, evidence, timestamps, analytics, and recent activity.

Actual Wages oversight dashboard with organization-wide analytics.

  1. Use the company-wide metrics to identify incomplete, overdue, or near-departure requests.
  2. Open a request only after it unlocks.
  3. Inspect all department states and evidence relevant to your review.
  4. Complete your own department check. Add a concise note if needed.
  5. Upload evidence, enter your current password, and sign.

Notes are visible as part of the oversight and review record. Keep them factual and related to clearance; do not include passwords or unnecessary personal information.

Safe daily-use checklist

  • Confirm you are signed in under the correct name and department.
  • Search by employee number when names are similar.
  • Verify evidence before every consequential action.
  • Read confirmation messages; do not double-submit.
  • Log out when finished.
  • Report unexpected access or data exposure to IT immediately.
Workflow in one sentence: File the correct employee data, collect 13 valid departmental clearances, approve the evidence, then remove access—and only then treat the request as complete.
